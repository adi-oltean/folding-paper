  parametric proposal ({'body': [('le', 'i', 36)]}) on the SAME different instance: True

  Reading this: the literal fact is REJECTED by the mechanical verifier on the
  different instance (a soundness violation -- it would have clipped away genuinely
  reachable states), so it is never added to fold_set for that case; the analyzer
  simply falls back to baseline (no fold) there.  This is the "cannot overfit into
  unsoundness" claim, exhibited: a non-generalizing proposal costs a wasted
  verification attempt, never a false safety proof.  The parametric fact -- the one
  the training loop actually learns and caches -- verifies on both.
