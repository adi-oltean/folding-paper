FOOTNOTES

  [A] Baseline domain.  The BASELINE column is a textbook interval analysis:
      bitwise and/or/xor have no interval transfer and return TOP (they are folded
      exactly on singleton operands); shifts by a constant ARE precise, being
      multiplication/division by a power of two.  Any operation whose exact integer
      result may leave [0, 2^w) returns TOP -- the concrete semantics wraps, the
      abstract side refuses to guess.  Widening is to thresholds drawn from the
      program's and the fact's constants, followed by a standard descending pass.

  [B] Fact-only components.  The small-set component and the relational <= side
      table are NEVER synthesised by a transfer function -- they are populated only
      by injected facts, and propagate through copies, joins, meets and comparison
      edges.  The baseline therefore stays a plain interval analysis.

  [C] Mutants break the CHECK, never the pattern's validity condition, so the
      injected fact stays valid and injecting it stays sound; the mutant tests that
      the fold buys no free lunch.  Breaking the validity condition instead is the
      other failure direction and is reported separately in TABLE 5.

  [D] Approximations.  Where the fact this analyzer can express is weaker than the
      fact the paper describes, the weaker fact is what was injected:
      pattern 8  KnownBits approximated: the fact is the known-zero mask h & 0xF000 == 0; it is
                 injected as its interval consequence h <= 0x0FFF, which is exact here because
                 the known-zero bits are the top bits.
      pattern 11 bit-set approximated: the fact is the grown-only bit set {0,1,2,3}; it is
                 injected as its interval consequence f <= 15, exact because the supplied bits
                 are the low four.
      pattern 13 the exact image is the EVEN values of [0,254] (128 points); it is injected as
                 the image interval [0,254] the paper's transport lemma gives, not as the
                 128-element set.

  [E] Soundness.  For every program, every variant and every sweep point, the
      abstract state at each program point was checked to CONTAIN the complete
      concrete reachable set at that point, enumerated by the interpreter.

  [F] UNREACH is reported as its own verdict: an empty abstract state proves every
      check vacuously and is never counted as a discharge.

  [G] DBF denominators.  DESIGN.md defines DBF against "the tightest convex
      approximation".  For patterns 3, 14 and 16 that is what its closed form is a
      closed form for, and DBF(hull) = DBF(denom) in TABLE 2.  For patterns 1/2,
      5/6 and 13 the design's formula is a closed form for a DIFFERENT, larger
      denominator -- respectively the interval the analysis holds without the fact,
      the declared byte extent of the region, and the k-bit encoded space -- so
      both fractions are printed and each sweep says which one its formula matches.
      No formula was chosen after seeing the measurement: each derivation is in the
      comment above its sweep in examples.py, and --check compares against the
      denominator that comment declares.

All harness expectations hold: 16/16 verdict flips, 0/16 mutant flips with a
concrete counterexample behind every mutant, and containment of the enumerated
concrete reachable set at every point of every program.
